Quote to Order (Q2O) process
=================================


The quote-to-order process involves the conversion of a customer's request for a quotation into an actual sales order. Here are the key steps in the Quote-to-Order process:

1. Quotation Creation:

Generate a quotation in response to a customer's request, detailing the products or services requested, quantities, prices, and any applicable discounts or terms.

2. Quotation Review and Approval:

Review the quotation for accuracy, pricing consistency, and alignment with customer requirements.
Obtain necessary approvals before sending the quotation to the customer.

3. Quotation Delivery to Customer:

Send the quotation to the customer through the preferred communication channel (email, online portal, etc.).
Communicate terms, conditions, and validity periods specified in the quotation.

4. Customer Response and Order Placement:

Wait for the customer's response to the quotation.
Upon the customer's agreement to proceed, convert the accepted quotation into a sales order.

5. Sales Order Creation:

Enter the details from the accepted quotation into the system to create a sales order, reflecting the agreed-upon terms, quantities, prices, and delivery schedules.

6. Order Confirmation:

Confirm the sales order internally to ensure alignment with the customer's accepted quotation.
Send an order confirmation to the customer, acknowledging receipt and acceptance of the order.

7. Order Fulfillment:

Proceed with the necessary steps to fulfill the order, which may involve inventory allocation, production scheduling, or service provision.

8. Delivery or Service Provision:

Deliver the products or provide the services to the customer as per the agreed-upon terms and schedules outlined in the sales order.

9. Invoicing:

Create and issue an invoice based on the completed delivery or service provision reflected in the sales order.
Ensure the accuracy of the invoice, including item details, quantities, prices, taxes, and payment terms.

10. Accounts Receivable and Payment Processing:

Record the sale in the accounts receivable ledger.
Receive payments from the customer as per the agreed-upon payment terms outlined in the sales order.

11. Order Closure and Documentation:

Close the sales order upon successful delivery, invoicing, and payment.
Maintain documentation related to the transaction for future reference and audit purposes.


These steps ensure a smooth transition from the quotation phase to the creation and fulfillment of a sales order, leading to successful delivery or service provision to the customer. Adjustments to these steps may occur based on specific industry requirements or company policies.
