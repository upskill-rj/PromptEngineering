

# 🧠 What is Oracle Fusion Cloud CX?

Oracle Fusion CX (Customer Experience) is Oracle’s cloud-native CRM and customer engagement platform used to manage:

* Sales
* Marketing
* Customer service
* Commerce
* Loyalty
* Field service
* Customer analytics

It helps organizations improve:

* customer engagement
* sales performance
* digital marketing
* omnichannel customer experience

It is part of:

> Oracle Fusion Applications

and runs on:

> Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI).

---

# 🔹 Simple Definition

> Oracle Fusion CX is Oracle’s cloud-based customer experience and CRM platform that automates sales, marketing, service, and customer engagement processes using AI, analytics, workflow automation, and cloud-native integrations.
