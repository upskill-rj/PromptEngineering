Deal Management Process
=================================



Deal management involves the process of negotiating, structuring, and managing business deals or contracts. Here are the key steps in the Deal Management process:

1. Deal Identification:

Identify potential business opportunities or deals through market analysis, customer inquiries, or sales efforts.
Evaluate the viability and alignment of potential deals with the company's goals and objectives.

2. Lead Generation and Qualification:

Generate leads through marketing efforts, referrals, or networking.
Qualify leads by assessing their potential fit, needs, and ability to benefit from the proposed deal.

3. Initial Contact and Relationship Building:

Initiate contact with potential partners, clients, or stakeholders to establish rapport and discuss preliminary details of the proposed deal.

4. Deal Structuring and Proposal Preparation:

Define the scope, terms, and conditions of the proposed deal based on discussions and negotiations.
Prepare a comprehensive proposal outlining the deal structure, objectives, benefits, timelines, and financial terms.

5. Negotiation and Agreement:

Engage in negotiations with the involved parties to finalize terms, resolve differences, and reach a mutually acceptable agreement.
Review and revise proposals as necessary during the negotiation process.

6. Contract Drafting and Review:

Draft a formal contract or agreement that encapsulates the finalized terms and conditions agreed upon during negotiations.
Review the contract for accuracy, legal compliance, and alignment with the agreed-upon terms.

7. Approval and Signing:

Obtain necessary approvals from stakeholders or higher management for the finalized contract.
Arrange for the signing of the contract by authorized representatives of both parties.

8. Deal Execution and Implementation:

Implement the terms of the deal as outlined in the contract, initiating the activities or services agreed upon.
Coordinate with relevant departments or teams to ensure smooth execution and delivery.

9. Performance Monitoring and Management:

Monitor the progress and performance of the deal against agreed-upon objectives, timelines, and deliverables.
Address any issues or deviations from the agreed terms promptly.

10. Relationship Management:

Cultivate and maintain strong relationships with partners or clients throughout the duration of the deal.
Address concerns, provide support, and foster a collaborative working relationship.

11. Post-Deal Evaluation and Analysis:

Conduct a post-mortem evaluation of the deal's success, identifying strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement.
Gather feedback from stakeholders to inform future deal management strategies.

12. Renewal, Extension, or Termination:

Evaluate options for renewal, extension, or termination of the deal based on performance, market conditions, and business needs.
Engage in renegotiations or discussions for contract renewals or extensions as necessary.


Effective deal management involves strategic planning, strong negotiation skills, clear communication, and meticulous execution to ensure successful and mutually beneficial outcomes for all involved parties. Adjustments to these steps may occur based on the nature of the deal, industry, or specific business requirements.
