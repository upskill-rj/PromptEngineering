Procure to Pay (P2P) process
=================================

The procure-to-pay (P2P) process involves all the steps from sourcing and procurement to payment and reconciliation. Here are the key steps in the Procure-to-Pay process:

1. Identify Need and Requisition:

Determine the need for goods or services within the organization.
Create a requisition detailing the items or services required, quantities, specifications, and delivery timelines.

2. Supplier Identification and Selection:

Identify potential suppliers through a sourcing process or from an approved vendor list.
Evaluate suppliers based on criteria such as pricing, quality, reliability, and terms of service.

3. Purchase Order (PO) Creation:

Generate a purchase order (PO) once a supplier is selected and the terms are negotiated.
Include details such as item descriptions, quantities, prices, delivery dates, and payment terms.

4. Order Approval and Dispatch:

Obtain necessary approvals for the purchase order from relevant stakeholders.
Dispatch the approved purchase order to the selected supplier.

5. Goods Receipt and Inspection:

Receive the goods or services from the supplier as per the purchase order.
Conduct inspections to ensure the received items meet quality standards and match the order specifications.

6. Invoice Receipt and Validation:

Receive the supplier's invoice based on the goods/services delivered.
Validate the invoice against the purchase order, goods receipt, and contract terms to ensure accuracy.

7. Invoice Approval and Payment Processing:

Obtain approvals for the supplier's invoice from authorized personnel.
Process the invoice for payment based on agreed-upon terms (e.g., net 30 days).
Initiate payment to the supplier through various methods (check, electronic funds transfer, etc.).

8. Accounts Payable Recording:

Record the payment in the accounts payable ledger to track outstanding liabilities to suppliers.

9. Reconciliation and Reporting:

Reconcile invoices, payments, and purchase orders to ensure accuracy in financial records.
Generate reports on procurement metrics, spending patterns, and payment statuses.

10. Supplier Relationship Management:

Maintain communication and relationships with suppliers to address issues, negotiate contracts, and explore opportunities for collaboration or improvement.

11. Continuous Improvement:

Evaluate the efficiency of the P2P process and identify areas for improvement, such as streamlining approvals, reducing cycle times, or optimizing supplier relationships.


These steps ensure a systematic and efficient process for procuring goods and services, validating invoices, and making timely payments to suppliers while maintaining accurate financial records. Adjustments to these steps may occur based on specific industry requirements or company policies.
