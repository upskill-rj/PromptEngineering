# Stakeholder Management – Complete Overview

## What is Stakeholder Management?

Stakeholder Management is the process of identifying, analyzing, engaging, communicating with, and managing individuals or groups who can influence or are impacted by a project, program, product, or organizational initiative.

While **Stakeholder Engagement** focuses on communication and relationship-building, **Stakeholder Management** is broader and includes:

* Identification
* Analysis
* Prioritization
* Communication
* Conflict Resolution
* Expectation Management
* Governance
* Decision Support

For senior roles such as Project Manager, Program Manager, Product Manager, Solution Architect, Delivery Lead, and SAFe Leader, stakeholder management is often the difference between project success and failure.

---

# Why Stakeholder Management is Important

Even technically successful projects can fail if stakeholders are not aligned.

### Example

A cloud migration project may be technically perfect, but if:

* Business leaders are not informed,
* Finance teams are concerned about costs,
* Security teams reject compliance controls,

the project may be delayed or cancelled.

Good stakeholder management ensures:

* Alignment
* Support
* Funding
* Adoption
* Successful outcomes
